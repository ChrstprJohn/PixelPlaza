# PixelPlaza
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Initially the owner testing locally, with several guest tabs for multiplayer checks. Future visitors join a 10-player social plaza.
## Product Purpose
Choose a nickname and predefined character, enter a small shared space, walk and chat. Success is seeing another guest move and receiving their message both in chat and above their character.
## Operating Context
Local loopback frontend/backend; shared VM Redis and Centrifugo. Landing page leads to character/name selection and lobby. Backend deployment is deferred.
## Capabilities and Constraints
One 10-player lobby, temporary guest sessions, no saved chat or movement history, authoritative server movement. User approved the supplied cozy daytime pixel-art map/chat reference. Four predefined characters: cat, sprout, mushroom and robot.
## Brand Commitments
PixelPlaza; cozy daytime plaza and readable quiet chat panel. The approved mockup is visual authority.
## Product Principles
Keep entry short, the world central, credentials backend-only and guest actions validated. Show live status and recoverable errors honestly.
